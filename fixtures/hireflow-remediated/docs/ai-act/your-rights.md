# Your rights when this system is used about you

Published on the careers site alongside the candidate notice.

## Right to an explanation — Article 86

If a screening decision was taken about you on the basis of this system's
output, you can ask us for a clear and meaningful explanation of **the role the
system played in the decision procedure** and **the main elements of the
decision taken**. Write to privacy@hireflow.example; we answer within thirty
days.

We store the reason codes with each decision precisely so that the answer is a
record rather than a reconstruction.

## Right to lodge a complaint — Article 85

If you consider that this system infringes the AI Act, you have the right to
submit a complaint to the **market surveillance authority** of your Member
State, which handles it under its Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 procedures. In
Ireland that is the National Standards Authority of Ireland.

You do not have to come to us first. We would rather you did, and we would
rather answer the Article 86 request than have the authority ask us why we did
not — but the right is yours either way.

## Right to human intervention — GDPR Article 22(3)

Every adverse outcome already goes to a named recruiter before it reaches you.
You can ask for that review to be repeated by a different person.
