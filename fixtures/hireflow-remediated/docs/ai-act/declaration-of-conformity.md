# EU declaration of conformity

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 47 and Annex V.

The signed, machine-readable declaration is at
[`conformity/declaration-of-conformity.json`](../../conformity/declaration-of-conformity.json).
Article 47(1) requires it to be machine-readable as well as written, which is
why the JSON is the authoritative copy and this page is the pointer to it.

| Article 47 requirement | Where |
|---|---|
| Written, machine-readable, signed | `conformity/declaration-of-conformity.json` |
| Identifies the system | `system.name`, `system.version` |
| States conformity with Chapter III Section 2 | `statement` |
| Annex V information | `provider`, `conformityAssessment`, `signature` |
| Kept available to authorities for ten years | `retention` |
| Single declaration covering other Union law | `otherUnionLegislation` — GDPR |

**Article 47(4):** drawing this up is the act by which the provider assumes
responsibility for compliance. It is kept up to date; a substantial
modification under Article 3(23) reopens the assessment behind it and this
document is redrawn.
