# Serious incident reporting procedure

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 73.

## Deadlines

| Situation | Deadline | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Widespread infringement, or an Art. 3(49)(b) incident | Immediately, **not later than 2 days** | Art. 73(3) |
| Death of a person | Immediately, **not later than 10 days** | Art. 73(4) |
| All other serious incidents | Immediately, **in any event not later than 15 days** | Art. 73(2) |

The clock starts at **awareness**, not confirmation.

## Authority

Netherlands: Rijksinspectie Digitale Infrastructuur (RDI), as market
surveillance authority. Contact on file with the compliance lead.

## Procedure

1. Detect — on-call alert, deployer report or candidate complaint.
2. Triage within 24 hours; assign severity and the applicable deadline.
3. **Preserve.** Article 73(6): do not alter the system in a way that may affect
   the later evaluation of causes. Freeze the model and prompt version and the
   relevant audit-log range.
4. Report.
5. Investigate, including a risk assessment and corrective action.
6. Correct under Article 20; inform deployers and distributors.
