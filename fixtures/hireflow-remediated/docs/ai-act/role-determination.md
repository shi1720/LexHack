# Role under the EU AI Act

HireFlow B.V. **develops** an AI system and places it on the market **under its
own name**. On the Article 3(3) definition, HireFlow is therefore the
**provider** of an AI system — not merely a deployer of OpenAI's model.

OpenAI remains the provider of the general-purpose AI model and carries the
Chapter V obligations for it. Those are different objects and both sets of
duties exist at the same time.

Because the system is used for the recruitment and selection of natural persons,
it falls within **Annex III, point 4(a)** and is high-risk under Article 6(2).
Article 25(1)(c) reaches the same result independently: directing a
general-purpose AI system at an Annex III use case makes the party who does so
the provider of a high-risk AI system.

## Article 25(2) — documentation from the model supplier

OpenAI's usage policies restrict certain high-risk uses. We have not obtained a
written undertaking that the Article 25(2) cooperation duty applies to us, so we
plan the Annex IV documentation on the assumption that we produce it ourselves.
