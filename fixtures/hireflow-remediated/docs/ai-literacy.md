# AI literacy and usage policy

Article 4 of the EU AI Act asks providers and deployers to take measures to
support the development of AI literacy among the people who operate their AI
systems. This is ours.

## What every recruiter is told before they get access

1. **What the score is.** A prioritisation signal produced by a language model
   comparing a resume to a posting. It is not a measure of the person.
2. **What it cannot do.** It cannot read between the lines, it cannot assess
   potential, and it is less accurate on short resumes, non-English resumes and
   career changers.
3. **Automation bias.** The known failure mode is deferring to the score. That is
   why HireFlow makes you record a view before revealing it.
4. **Your override is real and it is logged.** Use it. A high override rate is a
   signal about the model, not about you.
5. **What to do when something looks wrong.** Raise it in #hireflow-quality; it
   goes into the post-market monitoring review.

Refreshed annually and on every substantial change to the system.
